## Why Accuracy can be misleading?

### Precision 
- How precision/correct are positive predictions?
- used in Spam filter (dont block real emails)

### Recall 
- How many real positives did we recall/find?
- used in Medical AI (Dont miss any patient)

> Understanding model mistakes intelligently.
